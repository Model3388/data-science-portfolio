# Customer Segmentation Using Clustering Algorithms

## Overview

Clustering is an unsupervised machine learning technique used to identify patterns and group similar data points. This project explores how clustering can be applied to customer data to improve business decision-making.

## Business Use Case

An organization aims to better understand its customer base in order to improve marketing strategies and product development. This case study focuses on Apple Inc. and its iPhone product line.

## Input Data

The model would use the following customer attributes:

* Age
* Income level
* Education level
* Spending behavior
* Geographic location

These features were selected because they are commonly used in market segmentation and are known to influence purchasing behavior.

## Methodology

Agglomerative Hierarchical Clustering (AHC) is proposed as the modeling approach. This method groups data points based on similarity and builds clusters step by step.

## Expected Clusters

The model is expected to generate three primary clusters:

1. High-income, high-spending consumers
2. Middle-income, moderate-spending consumers
3. Lower-income, budget-conscious consumers

## Business Value

These clusters would allow the organization to:

* Tailor marketing campaigns to specific demographics
* Improve product positioning
* Increase efficiency in advertising spend

## Considerations

* Cluster quality depends on feature selection and data quality
* Results require interpretation and validation
* Business decisions should not rely solely on clustering outputs

## Conclusion

Clustering provides a powerful tool for understanding customer behavior and improving strategic decision-making. When applied correctly, it can significantly enhance marketing effectiveness and business performance.
