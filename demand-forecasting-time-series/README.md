# 📈 Demand Forecasting Using Time Series Modeling

## 📌 Overview

This project analyzes historical demand data to forecast future values using time series techniques. It demonstrates how past trends can be leveraged to make informed predictions about future demand.

## 🧠 Problem Statement

A business aims to forecast future demand for a product based on historical observations. The goal is to identify patterns in the data and generate a reasonable prediction for upcoming periods.

## 📊 Approach

A time series of demand values was analyzed over multiple time periods.

Key steps:

* Visualized demand trends over time
* Identified overall growth patterns
* Applied a simple forecasting approach using historical trends

## 📈 Key Findings

* Demand shows an overall upward trend
* Recent values suggest continued growth
* No strong seasonal pattern was identified within the observed window

## 🔮 Forecast

Based on the observed trend, demand is expected to continue increasing in the near future. A projected estimate for the next period was calculated using recent growth behavior.

## ⚠️ Limitations

* Limited data points reduce forecasting accuracy
* No seasonality or external factors included
* Assumes past trends will continue

## 💡 Business Insight

Even simple time series models can provide valuable guidance for:

* Inventory planning
* Resource allocation
* Anticipating future demand
