# Call Center Wait Time Analysis Using Probability

## Overview

This project explores how probability distributions can be used to analyze and improve customer wait times in a call center environment.

## Scenario

Lucian Telecom is seeking to reduce the time customers spend waiting to speak with a support agent.

Current system data indicates:

* Mean wait time: 8 minutes
* Standard deviation: 2 minutes

This suggests that wait times follow a normal distribution.

## Probabilistic Questions

### 1. What is the probability that a customer waits longer than 10 minutes?

This question helps quantify how often customers experience long delays and whether current performance meets service expectations.

### 2. What percentage of customers are served within an acceptable threshold (e.g., under 6 minutes)?

This allows the organization to measure how frequently it meets its service targets and identify performance gaps.

### 3. How would changes in staffing levels impact the distribution of wait times?

This question links operational decisions (staffing) to expected outcomes, enabling predictive improvements.

## Methodological Insight

These questions can be explored using:

* Normal distribution analysis
* Standardization (Z-scores)
* Regression modeling to incorporate additional variables such as staffing levels or time of day

## Business Impact

By applying probabilistic thinking, the company can:

* Set realistic performance benchmarks
* Improve customer satisfaction
* Optimize staffing and operations

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how probability distributions can be used to transform raw operational data into actionable business insights.
