# 📞 Call Center Wait Time Analysis Using Probability Distributions

## 📌 Overview

This project applies probability theory and normal distribution concepts to analyze and improve customer wait times in a call center environment. It demonstrates how probabilistic thinking can guide operational decision-making.

## 🧠 Problem Statement

Lucian Telecom aims to reduce customer wait times for online support. Current system data shows:

* Average wait time: 8 minutes
* Standard deviation: 2 minutes

The goal is to better understand and predict delays in order to improve customer service performance.

## 📊 Approach

A normal distribution model was used to represent customer wait times. The analysis focuses on:

* Understanding variability in wait times
* Identifying probabilities of long wait durations
* Framing actionable, data-driven questions

## ❓ Key Probabilistic Questions

1. **What is the probability that a customer waits longer than a target threshold (e.g., 10 minutes)?**
   → Helps quantify how often service levels are being missed

2. **What percentage of customers are served within an acceptable time window (e.g., under 6 minutes)?**
   → Measures service performance against internal goals

3. **How would changes in staffing levels impact the distribution of wait times?**
   → Supports operational decisions and resource allocation

## 💡 Business Insight

By framing the problem probabilistically, the organization can:

* Set measurable service targets
* Identify performance gaps
* Make informed staffing and process improvements

## 🛠️ Skills Demonstrated

* Probability distributions (normal distribution)
* Statistical reasoning
* Translating data into operational insights
* Business problem framing
